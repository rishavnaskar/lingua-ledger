Title:

Lingua Ledger: Multilingual Document AI You Can Trust (DataNova 2026, DN-AI-01)

Description:

Lingua Ledger pulls data from invoices, receipts, forms, and contracts in any language. It gives every field an honest confidence score, so you only have to review the details that actually need a human eye.

Here is a quick look at how the live app works:
- Each document is read twice by separate readers.
- An arbiter steps in on disagreements, and rules double-check math and formatting.
- Only low-confidence fields get flagged for manual review.
- The system catches duplicate uploads and refits its confidence scores after every review decision.

Tested on 198 public benchmark documents in nine languages:
- Calibration error dropped from 0.190 to 0.037
- Runs at about $5.64 per 1,000 documents
